# Entrakeria Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-26_

Entrakeria is a workout tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

- Your week plan, workout logs, and notes are stored only on your device (and on your own iPhone and Watch, which sync with each other), in the app's private storage and App Group container.
- Health data (workouts, heart rate, energy) is read from and written to Apple Health only with your permission. It is used only to show your stats and fill your log. It is never sent off the device, sold, or used for advertising.

## Network requests

- **Hike maps:** when you search for a place, the place name is sent to Apple Maps search (MapKit). Elevation tiles are downloaded from public terrain-tile servers (AWS Terrain Tiles / Terrarium) to draw the contour map. Those requests contain only the map tile coordinates.
- Nothing else is sent to any server.

## Children

Entrakeria does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
